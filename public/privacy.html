Privacy Policy & Cookie Disclosures

Last Updated: October 2026 • Published by DJ Tech NYC

1. Overview & Commitment

At DJ Tech NYC (operators of djtechnyc.com, park.djtechnyc.com, and parkusa.djtechnyc.com), we are committed to transparent, privacy-first civic utility tools.

This policy details how we treat information across our websites and web applications. We do not sell user personal data, require user registration, or store personally identifiable information on our servers.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Disclosures

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements across our websites. In accordance with Google's Advertising Policies and requirements for publishers:

  • Third-Party Vendors & Cookies: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites across the Internet.
  • DoubleClick / DART Cookie: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising: Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative at AboutAds.info or Network Advertising Initiative Opt-Out.
Transparency Note: Cookies do not contain sensitive personal details such as your name, email, or home address. You can also disable or clear cookies at any time via your browser settings.

3. Geolocation & Device Location Data

Our street parking utilities (StreetPark NYC and StreetParkingUSA) offer location-aware parking rule calculation.

  • Client-Side Only: When you click the Locate Me button, your browser requests permission to access your device's GPS coordinates. This computation occurs entirely locally in your device's browser memory.
  • No Tracking / No History: We do not log, transmit, store, or sell your precise coordinates. Location permission can be revoked at any time through your browser's site settings.

4. Municipal Open Data & Third-Party Integrations

Our platforms process public GIS data provided by government agencies including NYC Open Data (NYC Department of Transportation CSCL dataset), State Departments of Transportation, and OpenStreetMap.

When accessing mobile meter payment options, users are redirected directly to official licensed municipal vendors (such as ParkNYC, ParkMobile, PayByPhone, Flowbird, or Passport Parking). All payment transactions occur exclusively within those third-party platforms under their respective privacy policies.
